# Office Guide

Practical information for working from the Northwind Labs offices.

## Opening hours and access

Offices are open from 07:00 to 21:00 on weekdays. Your badge opens the office doors; lost badges must be reported to reception on the same day. Visitors must be registered in the visitor system and accompanied at all times.

## Desk booking

Desks are shared and booked in the Workplace app up to two weeks ahead. Desks booked but not checked in by 10:30 are released for others. Leave your desk clear at the end of the day.

## Meeting rooms

Meeting rooms can be booked for up to four hours at a time. Rooms for more than 12 people need approval from the office manager.

## Parking and commuting

The office has a limited number of parking spaces, allocated by lottery every quarter. Bicycle storage and showers are available on the ground floor. A public transport pass is reimbursed up to $100 per month.

## Kitchen and events

Coffee, tea and fruit are free. Lunch is provided on Wednesdays. The social committee organises a team event every quarter.

## Health and safety

Report accidents and hazards to the office manager immediately. Fire drills take place twice a year; follow the instructions of the fire wardens.
