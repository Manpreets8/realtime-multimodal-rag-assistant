# Procurement Policy

This policy covers buying goods and services from suppliers on behalf of Northwind Labs. Personal expenses and travel are covered by the Travel and Expense Policy.

## Approval thresholds

Purchases up to $1,000 can be approved by your manager. Purchases between $1,000 and $10,000 also need approval from the budget owner in Finance. Purchases above $10,000 need three competing quotes and approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## Purchase orders

A purchase order must be raised in Coupa before the supplier starts work. Invoices without a matching purchase order are returned to the supplier. Finance pays approved invoices within 45 days.

## New suppliers

New suppliers must complete a security questionnaire before they receive access to company data. Suppliers that process personal data must sign a data processing agreement. Onboarding a new supplier usually takes two weeks.

## Software purchases

Software subscriptions are bought centrally by IT so licences can be reused. Do not buy software with a personal or corporate card; request it through the IT Service Desk instead.

## Gifts from suppliers

Employees may accept gifts from suppliers worth up to $50. More valuable gifts must be declined or reported to the People team.
