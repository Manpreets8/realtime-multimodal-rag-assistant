# Travel and Expense Policy

Northwind Labs reimburses reasonable business expenses. This policy explains how to book travel, what is covered, and how to claim expenses.

## Booking travel

All flights and hotels must be booked through TripDesk, the company travel portal. Bookings made elsewhere are reimbursed only if TripDesk had no availability.

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more, with approval from a Vice President.

## Hotels

Hotel costs are capped at $200 per night. In New York, London and San Francisco the cap is $275 per night. Upgrades and minibar charges are not covered.

## Meals

The meal allowance (per diem) is $60 per day for domestic travel and $80 per day for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Mileage

Business use of a personal car is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. Commuting between home and your usual office is not business travel.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports in Expensify within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are required for every expense over $25. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year may request a corporate card from Finance.
